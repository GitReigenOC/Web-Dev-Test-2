Item: Sukuna's Finger

Description: A Sukuna Finger is a legendary, special-grade cursed object from the anime and manga series Jujutsu Kaisen. It is one of the twenty severed, indestructible fingers of Ryomen Sukuna, the undisputed King of Curses. Gross, wax-fleshed, and dripping with potent, malevolent cursed energy, swallowing even a single finger can grant immense power-or result in instant death unless the host possesses the rare trait to act as Sukuna's vessel.

Extra Details

Effects and Impacts • Unlocks Massive Cursed Energy: Eating a finger instantly transforms the user into a powerful curse or explodes them from pure energy overload.

• Resurrects the King of Curses: If a rare compatible vessel like Yuji Itadori swallows one, Ryomen Sukuna can incarnate and temporarily take control of the body.

• Attracts Dangerous Curses: The intense malevolent aura acts as a beacon, drawing hostile, high-level curses to its location. Current Status and Owners

• Yuji Itadori: Consumed the vast majority of the fingers throughout the series to suppress Sukuna's power.

• Ryomen Sukuna: Reclaimed and absorbed his own fingers to restore his full, prime special-grade strength.

• Jujutsu High: Stored several fingers in their heavily enchanted cursed warehouses before they were stolen by Mahito's group.
